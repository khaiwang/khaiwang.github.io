Hi! Welcome to my website.

My name is Zikai Wang. I am a 3rd year PhD student in Khoury College of Computer Sciences at Northeastern University, advised by Prof. [Cheng Tan](https://naizhengtan.github.io/) and Prof. [David Bau](https://baulab.info/). I am a member of the [System Research Group](https://srg.khoury.northeastern.edu/) and of the [NDIF](https://ndif.us/) (National Deep Inference Fabric) team.

I build infrastructure for interpretability research. My work gives researchers white-box access to large models: programmable reads and edits of model internals such as activations and logits, during inference, at the scale of production serving engines. My current project, [Model Intervention as a Service](/publications/5/), makes model intervention a capability of production inference engines, so that interpretability methods run on frontier-scale models while the engine keeps serving.

Before Northeastern, I obtained my M.E and B.E in Renmin University of China (RUC) under the supervision of Prof. Yunpeng Chai. Where I worked on the distributed database system.
